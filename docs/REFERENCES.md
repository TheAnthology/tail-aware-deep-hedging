# References and attribution

- Buehler, H., Gonon, L., Teichmann, J., and Wood, B. (2019). *Deep Hedging*. Quantitative Finance. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03042). Market/framework reference; this repository is an independent adapted implementation, not the authors' official code.
- [QuantLib](https://www.quantlib.org/): Heston pricing and the model-hedge reference.
- [TensorFlow](https://www.tensorflow.org/) and [Keras](https://keras.io/): neural policies and automatic differentiation.
- [NumPy](https://numpy.org/) and [SciPy](https://scipy.org/): simulation, calibration and numerical evaluation.
- [OpenIntro S&P500 data](https://www.openintro.org/data/index.php?data=sp500_1950_2018), upstream Yahoo Finance: historical source snapshot.

No third-party repositories or paper PDFs are vendored. Dependency licenses and data-source terms remain with their respective owners. `source_provenance.json` records hashes of the independent research-workspace files from which retained numerical kernels were extracted; private machine paths are omitted.
